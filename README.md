# Financial Sales Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI** dashboard that analyses sales, profit and units sold across countries, products and customer segments.

![Dashboard preview](Dashboard%20Preview.png)

## Key figures

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Total sales | 118.73M |
| Total units sold | 1.13M |
| Total profit | 16.89M |
| Number of countries | 5 |

## What the dashboard shows

- **Country-wise sales** and **country-wise profit** for Canada, France, Germany, Mexico and the United States
- **Product-wise profit** and **units sold by product** for Amarilla, Carretera, Montana, Paseo, Velo and VTT
- **Segment-wise sales** for Channel Partners, Enterprise, Government, Midmarket and Small Business
- **Month-wise sales** trend
- **Filters** for country, product and segment. Selecting a filter updates every chart and figure on the page.

## How to use it

1. Download `finance dashboard.pbit` and `sales_raw_data.xlsx`.
2. Open the `.pbit` file in **Power BI Desktop**.
3. If Power BI asks for the data source, choose `sales_raw_data.xlsx`.
4. Click the country, product or segment filters to explore the data.
