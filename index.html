Financial Sales Dashboard

Created by Sana Naik

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyses sales, profit and units sold across countries, products and customer segments. In total it covers sales of 118.73M, 1.13M units sold and a profit of 16.89M across 5 countries.

What the dashboard shows

  • Total sales, total units sold, total profit and the number of countries at a glance
  • Country-wise sales and country-wise profit for Canada, France, Germany, Mexico and the United States
  • Product-wise profit and units sold for Amarilla, Carretera, Montana, Paseo, Velo and VTT
  • Segment-wise sales for Channel Partners, Enterprise, Government, Midmarket and Small Business
  • Month-wise sales trend
  • Filters for country, product and segment, which update every chart
